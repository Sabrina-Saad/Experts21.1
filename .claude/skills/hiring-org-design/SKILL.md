# Hiring & org design

Plans the team by phase, writes job descriptions, sets compensation bands and designs the interview process.

**Skill ID:** SK-27 · See `docs/expert-roster.md` for the agents that use this skill.

## When to use

Use this skill when planning headcount, writing a job post, or deciding what to hire vs. contract vs. automate with agents.

## Procedure

1. Start from the phase plan in `docs/06` (founding → seed 8–12 FTE → growth).
2. For each role decide: hire, contract, or agent-first with a human owner.
3. Write the job description with outcomes for the first 90 days.
4. Set salary bands (validate with current market data) and fully-loaded cost (+20–30%).
5. Design a structured interview with a scoring rubric.

## Output

- Org chart by phase.
- Job description.
- Comp band and loaded cost.
- Interview plan.

## Project context

- Hiring priorities in `docs/06`: lead engineer, content lead, creator/social marketer, support Trip Guide.

## Guardrails

- Use fair, job-related criteria; follow employment law (human HR/legal sign-off).
- Follow the team ground rules in `CLAUDE.md` (cite sources, label estimates, escalate safety issues to a human).
