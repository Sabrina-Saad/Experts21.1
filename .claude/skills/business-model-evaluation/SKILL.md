# Business model & strategy evaluation

Compares business models (affiliate, partner booking, merchant of record) on revenue, risk and regulatory weight.

**Skill ID:** SK-07 · See `docs/expert-roster.md` for the agents that use this skill.

## When to use

Use this skill when choosing or changing how the company makes money or sells travel, or evaluating a strategic option.

## Procedure

1. List the options. Default set: A) content + subscription + affiliate links; B) sell via a partner (Duffel / host agency) as agent; C) merchant of record with own ARC accreditation.
2. For each, score revenue potential, margin, capital needs, regulatory weight (`docs/05`), operational load, and risk (`docs/08`).
3. Identify the triggers that would justify moving from one model to the next (e.g., monthly bookings, conversion rate, cash on hand).
4. Recommend one option with the trade-offs stated plainly.

## Output

- Options table with scores and one-line rationale each.
- Recommendation + triggers for the next phase.

## Project context

- Current recommendation: launch on Model A; add Model B (Duffel) in phase 2; avoid Model C early (`docs/04`, `docs/05`).

## Guardrails

- Regulatory conclusions need sign-off from travel-regulatory-counsel and a human lawyer.
- Follow the team ground rules in `CLAUDE.md` (cite sources, label estimates, escalate safety issues to a human).
