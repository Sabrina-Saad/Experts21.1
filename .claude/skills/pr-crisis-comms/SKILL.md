# PR, media relations & crisis communications

Writes press releases, runs the data-led PR survey, handles rapid-response explainers and crisis statements.

**Skill ID:** SK-44 · See `docs/expert-roster.md` for the agents that use this skill.

## When to use

Use this skill when planning press, responding to news (rule changes, disruptions), or handling a crisis.

## Procedure

1. Proactive: annual 'First-Time Traveler Fear Index' survey; story angles from real data.
2. Rapid response: when entry rules change (ETIAS, EES, UK ETA, passport fees), publish a clear explainer within 24 hours.
3. Crisis: confirm facts first, put traveler safety first, say what we know and what we're doing, update regularly, one spokesperson.
4. Keep a media list and a holding-statement library.

## Output

- Press release or pitch.
- Explainer draft.
- Crisis statement + Q&A.

## Project context

- PR plan in `docs/07`; crisis-relevant risks in `docs/08`.

## Guardrails

- Never speculate in a crisis; legal reviews statements about incidents.
- Follow the team ground rules in `CLAUDE.md` (cite sources, label estimates, escalate safety issues to a human).
