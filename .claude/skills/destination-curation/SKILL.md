# Destination curation (touristy vs. off-the-path)

Selects and vets places, writes first-timer guides and applies responsible-travel rules.

**Skill ID:** SK-13 · See `docs/expert-roster.md` for the agents that use this skill.

## When to use

Use this skill when choosing destinations for first-timers, or writing/reviewing 'touristy' and 'off-the-path' recommendations.

## Procedure

1. Score a destination for first-timers: cost, flight access from U.S. hubs, safety, English prevalence, ease of transport, tourism infrastructure.
2. For each city, build two lists: **Touristy (and worth it)** and **Off-the-path (and safe)**, each with why, cost, how to get there, and best time.
3. Vet off-the-path picks with a local contributor: safe to reach, legal, welcoming to visitors, not a private residence or fragile site.
4. Add first-timer notes: etiquette, tipping, scams to avoid, accessibility.
5. Set a refresh date.

## Output

- Destination scorecard.
- Two-list city guide with logistics and a 'last verified' date.

## Project context

- Starter cities named in `docs/02`: Mexico City, Lisbon, London, Tokyo, San José CR. Plan is 20 launch cities with one vetted local contributor each (`docs/06`).

## Guardrails

- Avoid over-tourism harm (risk #11): no fragile or residential spots, respect local caps and rules.
- Follow the team ground rules in `CLAUDE.md` (cite sources, label estimates, escalate safety issues to a human).
