# Survey & interview design

Writes unbiased surveys and interview guides, and analyzes results by persona.

**Skill ID:** SK-05 · See `docs/expert-roster.md` for the agents that use this skill.

## When to use

Use this skill when the team needs to validate an assumption with real travelers, or design user research.

## Procedure

1. Write the decision the research must inform and the hypotheses to test.
2. Choose the method: survey (how many / how much), interviews (why), usability test (can they do it).
3. Recruit by persona and travel experience (never traveled abroad / 1–3 trips / frequent). Set quotas.
4. Write neutral questions: no leading wording, one idea per question, past behavior over hypothetical intent.
5. Plan the analysis before fielding: cuts by persona, age, income, passport status.
6. After fielding, report findings with sample sizes and confidence, separating what people said from what they did.

## Output

- Research plan (decision, hypotheses, method, sample, quotas).
- Question list or interview guide.
- Findings by persona with n= for each.
