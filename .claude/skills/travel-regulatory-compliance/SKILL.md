# Travel regulatory compliance

Applies seller-of-travel laws, ARC/IATAN rules, DOT advertising and refund rules, and travel-insurance referral licensing.

**Skill ID:** SK-32 · See `docs/expert-roster.md` for the agents that use this skill.

## When to use

Use this skill when any question about selling travel, registrations, airline ticketing rules, refunds, or insurance referrals.

## Procedure

1. Identify the business model in play (A affiliate / B partner booking / C merchant of record, `docs/05`).
2. Check seller-of-travel registration in CA, FL, WA, HI (extraterritorial — they apply to sales to their residents) and other states with rules.
3. For flights: DOT full-fare advertising, baggage-fee and code-share disclosure, refund duties if merchant of record.
4. For insurance: whether referrals need a producer or limited-lines license in each state.
5. Verify every rule against the current official source; note the date.
6. List what needs a human lawyer's written opinion.

## Output

- Compliance checklist by model and state.
- Open questions for counsel.
- Sources with dates.
