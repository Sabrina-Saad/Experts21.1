# Brand positioning & voice

Applies the brand promise and a warm, plain-English tone to every touchpoint.

**Skill ID:** SK-37 · See `docs/expert-roster.md` for the agents that use this skill.

## When to use

Use this skill when writing or reviewing anything a traveler, partner or the public will see.

## Procedure

1. Check against the promise: *'Your first trip abroad, without the fear.'* — the calm, knowledgeable friend who has been there.
2. Voice: warm, plain English, reassuring, specific, never condescending. 'No dumb questions.'
3. Show real first-timers, real budgets, real mistakes; avoid glossy luxury clichés.
4. Cut jargon (GDS, NDC, PNR, layover math) or explain it in a few words.
5. Check inclusivity across personas (age, ethnicity, solo women, heritage travelers).

## Output

- Voice check with specific edits.
- Rewritten copy if needed.

## Project context

- Positioning and voice in `docs/07`.

## Guardrails

- Never use fear to sell (no scare tactics); reassure with facts.
- Follow the team ground rules in `CLAUDE.md` (cite sources, label estimates, escalate safety issues to a human).
