# Partnership development & negotiation

Prospects, pitches and negotiates B2B2C, affiliate and co-marketing deals.

**Skill ID:** SK-43 · See `docs/expert-roster.md` for the agents that use this skill.

## When to use

Use this skill when finding, pitching or negotiating with a partner.

## Procedure

1. Pick partner types from `docs/07`: universities, credit unions/banks, employers, passport facilities/libraries, heritage orgs, senior orgs, language apps, eSIM, insurance.
2. Define what each side gets (value exchange) and the success metric.
3. Prepare the pitch: their member problem, our solution, proof, simple pilot.
4. Negotiate: economics, exclusivity (avoid early), data sharing (privacy), brand use, term.
5. Hand the term sheet to `contract-drafting-review`.

## Output

- Partner target list with priority.
- Pitch outline.
- Term sheet points.

## Project context

- Affiliate economics in `docs/04`.

## Guardrails

- No data sharing without privacy review.
- Follow the team ground rules in `CLAUDE.md` (cite sources, label estimates, escalate safety issues to a human).
