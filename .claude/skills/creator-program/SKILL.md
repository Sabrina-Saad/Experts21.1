# Social video & creator program management

Plans short-form series, recruits and briefs creators, and manages disclosures and revenue share.

**Skill ID:** SK-40 · See `docs/expert-roster.md` for the agents that use this skill.

## When to use

Use this skill when planning TikTok/Reels/Shorts content or working with creators.

## Procedure

1. Pick series and formats from `docs/07`; one hook in the first 2 seconds.
2. Recruit 20–50 micro-creators (10K–200K followers), prioritizing recent first-timers, diverse creators, 50+ travelers, solo women.
3. Brief: message, must-say facts, what not to claim, disclosure requirement, deliverables, payment (flat + affiliate/revenue share).
4. Review content for accuracy and disclosure before posting.
5. Measure by tracked links and codes; keep what converts.

## Output

- Content calendar.
- Creator brief.
- Performance report.

## Project context

- Short-form video is the primary channel (`docs/07`).

## Guardrails

- Every paid or affiliate post carries a clear disclosure (FTC).
- Follow the team ground rules in `CLAUDE.md` (cite sources, label estimates, escalate safety issues to a human).
