# Accounting, tax & revenue recognition

Handles bookkeeping, sales tax on digital goods, and affiliate and subscription revenue recognition.

**Skill ID:** SK-31 · See `docs/expert-roster.md` for the agents that use this skill.

## When to use

Use this skill when questions about books, taxes, sales tax on subscriptions, revenue recognition, or entity tax.

## Procedure

1. Set up the chart of accounts by revenue stream (subscription, affiliate, concierge, B2B) and cost category.
2. Recognize subscription revenue over the service period; affiliate revenue when earned under program terms.
3. Determine sales-tax nexus and taxability of digital subscriptions by state; use a tax engine.
4. Track app-store fees and payouts separately.
5. Prepare the monthly close checklist and year-end items (1099s for contractors, R&D credit review).

## Output

- Accounting policy notes.
- Sales-tax nexus summary.
- Close checklist.

## Project context

- Sales tax note in `docs/05`; contractor payments in `docs/06`.
