# Contract drafting & review

Drafts and reviews ToS, partner and affiliate agreements, contributor IP assignments and vendor contracts.

**Skill ID:** SK-35 · See `docs/expert-roster.md` for the agents that use this skill.

## When to use

Use this skill when drafting or reviewing any agreement.

## Procedure

1. Identify the parties, the deal and our key risks.
2. Check the essentials: scope, payment, term and termination, IP ownership/assignment, confidentiality, data protection, liability caps, indemnities, governing law.
3. Flag non-standard or one-sided terms and explain the risk in plain English.
4. Propose redlines and fallback positions.
5. List items for human counsel.

## Output

- Issues list ranked by risk.
- Proposed redlines.
- Plain-English summary.

## Project context

- Contributor agreements need IP assignment, accuracy standards and conflict disclosure (`docs/06`).

## Guardrails

- Not legal advice; a licensed attorney signs off.
- Follow the team ground rules in `CLAUDE.md` (cite sources, label estimates, escalate safety issues to a human).
