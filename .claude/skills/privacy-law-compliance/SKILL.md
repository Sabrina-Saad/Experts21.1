# Privacy law compliance

Applies CCPA/CPRA and other state privacy laws, COPPA and GDPR; writes notices and handles data-subject requests.

**Skill ID:** SK-34 · See `docs/expert-roster.md` for the agents that use this skill.

## When to use

Use this skill when collecting, using, sharing or storing personal data, or writing privacy notices.

## Procedure

1. Map the data flow: what is collected, why, where stored, who it's shared with (including ad and affiliate partners).
2. Check obligations: notice at collection, opt-out of sale/sharing, sensitive data limits (passport, precise location), data-subject request handling.
3. Check age: no marketing to under-13s (COPPA); students persona is 18+.
4. Check GDPR if targeting EU residents or using EU contributors.
5. Update the privacy notice and records of processing.

## Output

- Data map.
- Obligations checklist.
- Draft notice text.
