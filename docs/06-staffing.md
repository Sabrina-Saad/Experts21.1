# 06 — Staffing Plan

> Principle: **small core team + network of freelance local experts.** Content accuracy and human reassurance are the product, so "local experts" and "traveler support" are core functions, not afterthoughts.

## Phase 0 — Founding team (months 0–6, pre-seed / bootstrapped)

| Role | Type | Responsibility | Est. cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Founder / CEO (product + brand) | FT | Vision, fundraising, partnerships, brand voice | Equity / low salary |
| Technical co-founder / lead engineer | FT | Architecture, fare-alert engine, app | Equity + $0–$120K |
| Content lead / travel editor | PT → FT | Guides, checklists, editorial standards, fact-checking | $50–$80K or contractor |
| Freelance writers & native-speaker reviewers | Contract | Destination guides, language packs | $150–$500 per guide/pack |
| Designer (UX + brand) | Contract | Calm, accessible UX (important for 50+ persona) | $60–$120/hr |
| Legal / accounting | Retainer | See `05-permits-and-licenses.md` | $300–$3K/mo |

## Phase 1 — Seed (months 6–24): ~8–12 FTE

| Function | Roles | U.S. salary range (approx.) |
|---|---|---|
| **Engineering** (3–4) | Full-stack/mobile ×2, backend/data (fares & APIs), ML/AI engineer (assistant, personalization) | $130K–$190K each |
| **Product & design** (1–2) | Product designer; PM (can be founder initially) | $120K–$170K |
| **Content & local expertise** (2) | Managing editor; destination/language content manager running a contributor network | $70K–$110K |
| **Growth & marketing** (2) | Growth/performance marketer; social & creator-partnerships manager | $80K–$140K |
| **Traveler support / "Trip Guides"** (1–2 + part-time) | Trained support agents who answer "I'm scared, what do I do?" chats; 24/7 emergency coverage via on-call rotation or outsourced partner | $45K–$65K (or BPO $15–$30/hr) |
| **Partnerships / BD** (1) | Affiliates, OTAs, insurance, universities, credit unions | $90K–$140K + commission |
| **Ops / finance** (fractional) | Fractional CFO/bookkeeper | $2K–$6K/mo |

**Fully-loaded cost:** add ~20–30% for benefits/payroll taxes. Seed-stage payroll ≈ **$1.0M–$1.8M/yr**. Remote-first hiring (and contractors in lower-cost regions for content/support) can cut 20–40%.

## Phase 2 — Growth (24+ months)

- Trust & safety lead (community content moderation, fake-review prevention).
- Compliance/legal counsel (in-house) if we become seller of travel.
- Data analyst.
- Regional content leads (Europe, LatAm, Asia).
- Human trip-planning concierge team (premium tier).
- B2B account managers (universities, employers).

## The "expert agents" layer

Since the team plans a set of **AI expert agents**, staff can be leaner if agents handle first-line work. Suggested agent roles mirroring this docs folder:

| Agent | Human owner | Use of this research |
|---|---|---|
| Market & research analyst | CEO | `01-market.md` |
| Customer insight / persona agent | PM / Growth | `02-target-customers.md` |
| Competitive intelligence agent | CEO / BD | `03-competitors.md` |
| Finance & unit-economics agent | Fractional CFO | `04-startup-and-operating-costs.md` |
| Compliance agent | Counsel | `05-permits-and-licenses.md` |
| Org & hiring agent | CEO | `06-staffing.md` |
| Marketing & content agent | Growth lead | `07-marketing.md` |
| Risk agent | CEO / Counsel | `08-risks.md` |
| Traveler-facing guide agent (product) | Support lead | All — with human escalation |

**Rule:** any traveler-facing AI must escalate to a human for emergencies, safety, medical and legal/visa edge cases, and must cite official sources (State Dept, embassy, CDC) for entry requirements.

## Hiring priorities (in order)
1. Lead engineer (fare engine + app).
2. Content lead with real international travel + editorial chops.
3. Creator/social marketer who understands TikTok/Reels travel content.
4. Support "Trip Guide" — empathetic, multilingual if possible.

## Contributor network design
- 1 vetted local contributor per launch city (start: 20 cities).
- Paid per guide + quarterly refresh fee.
- Native-speaker reviewer for each language pack (start: Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese, German, Japanese, Greek, Tagalog, Vietnamese, Korean).
- Contributor agreement: IP assignment, accuracy standards, disclosure of conflicts (no undisclosed paid placements).
