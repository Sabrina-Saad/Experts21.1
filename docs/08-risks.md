# 08 — Biggest Risks

Ranked by **likelihood × impact** for the recommended launch model (content + guidance + subscription + affiliate).

| # | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Customer acquisition too expensive vs. low ARPU** | High | High | Organic-first (SEO, creators, community, referrals); B2B2C partnerships; free fare alerts as hook; kill paid channels that miss LTV:CAC 3:1 |
| 2 | **Commoditization by free tools & AI** (Google Flights, ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini planners, OTA AI assistants) | High | High | Differentiate on trust, verified local content, human backup, community, and a full first-trip *program* rather than itineraries. Integrate AI ourselves |
| 3 | **Inaccurate guidance harms a traveler** (wrong visa/ETA rule, outdated safety info, bad advice in emergency) | Medium | Very high (safety, legal, brand) | "Last verified" dates; link official sources (State Dept, embassies, CDC); editorial review; human escalation for emergencies; E&O insurance; clear disclaimers |
| 4 | **Data/API dependency** — suppliers change terms or shut off (e.g., Amadeus Self-Service shut down July 2026) | Medium–High | High | Multi-provider abstraction layer; affiliates + Duffel + backups; don't build on a single free API |
| 5 | **Affiliate economics shift** — OTAs cut commissions, cookie windows shrink, AI agents bypass referral links | Medium | High | Diversify (subscription, B2B, concierge); direct partnerships with insurers/eSIM; own booking in phase 2 |
| 6 | **Travel demand shock** — pandemic, war, terrorism, recession, strong advisories, airline disruptions | Medium | High | Variable cost base; content pivots (domestic "practice trips," Mexico/Canada starters); subscription value not tied solely to booking |
| 7 | **Regulatory misstep** — SOT laws, auto-renewal laws (CA ARL), FTC endorsement/fake reviews, junk-fee total pricing, insurance-referral licensing, privacy | Medium | Medium–High | Counsel before each model change; compliant cancel flow; disclosure templates for creators; privacy-by-design for passport data |
| 8 | **Low willingness to pay** among young first-timers | High | Medium | Freemium; monetize via affiliates; target 50+ persona and B2B for subscription revenue |
| 9 | **Seasonality & low frequency** — first-timers travel 0–1×/yr; churn after trip | High | Medium | Annual plans; post-trip "next trip" engine; deal alerts keep engagement year-round; community |
| 10 | **Trust & safety in community/UGC** — fake reviews, scams, unsafe "hidden gem" recommendations, harassment | Medium | Medium–High | Moderation, verification, FTC fake-review compliance; never surface private residences/unsafe areas as "hidden gems" |
| 11 | **Over-tourism / ethical backlash** for pushing "off-the-path" spots | Low–Medium | Medium | Responsible-travel guidelines; spread demand; partner with local orgs; respect local rules (e.g., city tourist caps/fees) |
| 12 | **Entry-rule churn** (EU ETIAS repeatedly delayed, EES rollout, UK ETA fee hikes, passport fee/processing changes) | High | Low–Medium (opportunity if handled well) | Dedicated "rules desk" content owner; automated monitoring; push alerts to affected users |
| 13 | **Currency swings** (weaker dollar reduces outbound demand/affordability) | Medium | Medium | Budget-first destination finder steers toward value destinations |
| 14 | **App-store dependency & fees** (15–30%) | Medium | Medium | Web checkout where allowed; PWA; email ownership |
| 15 | **Key-person / execution risk** — small team, broad scope ("full travel system") | High | High | **Narrow MVP**: fare alerts + first-trip checklist + survival language for 20 destinations. Expand only after retention proves out |
| 16 | **Liability for in-trip incidents** if we offer human guides/meetups | Low–Medium | High | Contractor vetting, insurance, clear ToS; phase in carefully |

## Top 3 to watch (board-level)
1. **CAC vs. LTV** — the business model lives or dies here.
2. **AI commoditization** — make sure our value is *trust + human + verified content*, not "AI itinerary."
3. **Guidance accuracy & safety** — one viral story of a stranded customer due to bad info can sink the brand.

## Early-warning metrics
- Blended CAC trend; free→paid conversion; 90-day retention.
- % of guides past their refresh date.
- Affiliate EPC (earnings per click) by partner.
- Support tickets tagged "emergency" / "wrong info."
- API error rates / provider concentration (% of searches on one provider).
