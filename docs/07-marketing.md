# 07 — Marketing Strategy

> **Core message:** *"Your first trip abroad, without the fear."* We're the calm, knowledgeable friend who has been there — not another booking site.

## Positioning

- **For:** Americans who want to travel abroad but feel unprepared or nervous.
- **Who need:** one trusted place to go from "I want to go" to "I'm home and planning the next one."
- **We are:** an all-in-one travel guide — fare alerts, step-by-step checklists, survival language, touristy + off-the-path picks, and real humans if something goes wrong.
- **Unlike:** Expedia/Google Flights (tools for people who already know what they're doing) or Duolingo (months of lessons, not trip-ready phrases).

## Brand voice
Warm, plain-English, reassuring, never condescending. "No dumb questions." Show real first-timers, real budgets, real mistakes.

## Unit-economics constraint

Travel paid CAC is high and rising (~35% increase 2022→2025 per Adobe; mobile CPI ~$4–5). With blended ARPU of ~$20–$45/yr (see `04-startup-and-operating-costs.md`), **organic, community and creator channels must drive ≥ 60–70% of acquisition.**

## Channel plan

### 1. Short-form video (primary) — TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts
- Series ideas: *"First Time Abroad"* (follow a real first-timer), *"What $1,500 gets you in…"*, *"Things nobody tells you at customs"*, *"5 phrases that save you in Lisbon"*, *"Touristy vs. Local: Rome edition"*.
- Creator program: 20–50 micro-creators (10K–200K followers) on revenue share/affiliate + flat fee. Prioritize creators who are themselves recent first-timers, Black/Latino/Asian-American travel creators, 50+ travel creators, solo female travelers.

### 2. SEO & content hub (compounding)
- Programmatic + editorial pages: "Do I need a visa for [country] as an American?", "[City] for first-timers", "How to get from [airport] to city center", "ETIAS/ETA explained", "first international flight checklist".
- Every entry-requirement page shows **last-verified date** — trust signal and freshness advantage.
- Watch: AI search (Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT/Claude) reduces clicks — structure content to be cited (clear facts, schema markup) and build brand search.

### 3. Fare-deal email/push (retention + virality)
- Free deal alerts from home airport (Going-style) are the **top-of-funnel hook**; deals link to "plan this trip" flows.
- Shareable deal cards → group-trip invites.

### 4. Community
- Reddit presence (helpful, disclosed), Discord/in-app community, "Travel Mentor" program matching first-timers with experienced travelers.
- Post-trip stories → UGC → social proof.

### 5. Partnerships (B2B2C)
| Partner | Offer |
|---|---|
| Universities / study-abroad offices | Pre-departure program for students + parent dashboard |
| Credit unions, banks, card issuers | Member benefit: "First Trip Abroad" kit |
| Employers / HR benefits | Travel-readiness perk |
| Passport acceptance facilities, libraries, AAA-type clubs, community centers | Workshops: "Your first passport → first trip" |
| Heritage/cultural organizations | VFR trip programs, language packs |
| AARP-type senior orgs, retirement communities | Calm, guided travel for 50+ |
| Language apps (Babbel, Pimsleur), eSIM, insurance | Co-marketing + affiliate |

### 6. PR
- Annual "First-Time Traveler Fear Index" survey (original data → press coverage; Babbel/Promova/Squaremouth show this works).
- Timely explainers when rules change (ETIAS, EES, UK ETA, passport fee changes).

### 7. Paid (test, then scale only profitable)
- Meta/TikTok ads to lookalikes of converting users; Google Search on high-intent "first trip abroad" terms; Apple Search Ads.
- Retargeting from content visitors.

## Funnel & lifecycle

1. **Inspire** — social video, SEO "where can I go for $X".
2. **Capture** — free account: home airport + dream destinations → deal alerts.
3. **Activate** — personalized "Trip Readiness Score" & checklist (passport ✓, ETA ✓, insurance ✓…).
4. **Convert** — Premium: unlimited alerts, full language packs, offline guides, human chat. 7-day free trial.
5. **Book** — affiliate flows for flights, stays, eSIM, insurance, tours.
6. **In-trip** — offline phrasebook, emergency card, daily tips.
7. **Post-trip** — review, share story, refer a friend (give 1 month / get 1 month), plan next trip.

## KPIs

| Stage | KPI | Target (yr 1) |
|---|---|---|
| Acquisition | Blended CAC per active user | < $10–$15 |
| Activation | % new users completing trip setup | > 40% |
| Conversion | Free → paid | 5–10% |
| Monetization | Affiliate revenue per booked trip | $60+ |
| Retention | 12-month paid retention | > 50% |
| Referral | Viral coefficient | > 0.3 |
| Trust | NPS | > 50 |

## Seasonal calendar
- **Jan–Mar:** "Wave season," passport applications spike, summer-trip planning → biggest acquisition push. Passport processing-time content.
- **Apr–Jun:** Pre-departure content (checklists, language), summer deals.
- **Jul–Aug:** In-trip content; fall/shoulder-season deals.
- **Sep–Nov:** Holiday/winter sun, Japan/Europe shoulder deals; Black Friday subscription promo.
- **Dec:** Gift subscriptions ("Give someone their first trip").
