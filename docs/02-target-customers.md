# 02 — Target Customers

> **Who we serve:** Americans who *want* to travel internationally but feel unprepared, overwhelmed, or scared — first-timers and "infrequent, nervous" travelers. Not road warriors, not luxury travelers with a human advisor.

## Evidence on the pain

| Finding | Source |
|---|---|
| **69% of Gen Z travelers** have canceled or changed travel plans due to anxiety and lack of preparedness; ~half have gotten lost, ~25% missed a flight, 23% lost reservations | Babbel survey *(methodology not verified)* |
| **~1 in 3 American travelers** feel anxious about language barriers abroad; **Gen Z** reports this at a much higher rate — and also makes the most attempts to learn languages pre-trip | Promova study |
| Gen Z & Millennials report **higher flight anxiety** than older generations (June 2025) | Squaremouth |
| **Affordability and safety** are top priorities, especially for **first-time international travelers** (18–35, n≈1,100, Dec 2025) | EF Ultimate Break / Qualtrics |
| Among adults 50+, **cost** is the main obstacle to a first trip abroad; safety trails | AARP |
| Social-media inspiration creates "travel dysmorphia" — feeling one's trips don't measure up | Talker Research |
| Barriers rising: political/social unrest, natural disasters, war; Gen Z most concerned about environmental impact | WYSE Travel Confederation |

**Takeaway:** Fear is specific and solvable: *"Will I get lost? Will I get scammed? Can I afford it? Will I be able to communicate? What documents do I need? What if something goes wrong?"* — each maps to a product feature.

## Personas

### 1. "First Passport" Fiona — 22–30, first international trip
- Just got (or is getting) her first passport. Inspired by TikTok; budget $1,500–$3,000.
- **Fears:** looking stupid at the airport, getting scammed, running out of money, language.
- **Needs:** step-by-step checklist from passport → packing → landing; fare alerts for cheap routes; "first trip" destination suggestions (Mexico City, Lisbon, London, Tokyo, San José CR).
- **Willingness to pay:** low upfront; responds to freemium + $5/mo or ~$49/yr; high affiliate value.
- **Channels:** TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube, Reddit (r/travel, r/solotravel, r/TravelHacks).

### 2. "Finally Going" Gary & Gloria — 50–68, empty-nesters / new retirees
- Have money and time, little experience. Bucket list: Italy, Ireland, Greece, cruise add-ons.
- **Fears:** health issues abroad, falling ill, mobility, scams, tech complexity, getting stranded.
- **Needs:** large-type, calm UX; phone/chat human help; travel insurance guidance; slower-paced itineraries; accessibility notes.
- **Willingness to pay:** highest — $99–$199/yr or per-trip concierge ($149–$499).
- **Channels:** Facebook, YouTube, email newsletters, AARP-type partnerships, local libraries/community centers, podcasts.

### 3. "Heritage Seeker" Hector — 25–45, visiting family roots
- Often first-gen or second-gen; traveling to parents'/grandparents' country (Mexico, Philippines, India, Nigeria, Dominican Republic, Vietnam, Italy, Ireland).
- **Fears:** language gaps with relatives, documentation (dual citizenship, visas), safety in specific regions.
- **Needs:** language lessons focused on family conversation; visa/document guidance; flight deals on VFR (visiting friends & relatives) routes.
- **Channels:** community orgs, WhatsApp groups, culturally specific creators, Spanish-language media.

### 4. "Solo Brave" Sam — 25–40, solo traveler (often women)
- Wants to go alone, worried about safety.
- **Needs:** neighborhood-level safety intel, verified women-friendly stays, check-in/share-location features, community meetups, emergency info.
- **Channels:** Instagram, Reddit, solo-female-travel Facebook groups, podcasts.

### 5. "Student / Study-Abroad" Sophia — 18–23
- Semester abroad or post-grad trip; parents often co-decide (and pay).
- **Needs:** budget tools, rail passes, hostels, parent-facing safety dashboard.
- **Channels:** university study-abroad offices (B2B2C), campus ambassadors, TikTok.

### 6. "Off-the-Path Curious" Olivia — 28–45, has done 1–2 tourist trips
- Done Paris/Cancún; wants something "real" but is nervous about less-touristy places.
- **Needs:** our "Touristy vs. Local" toggle; vetted hidden gems; cultural etiquette; local-guide marketplace.

**Priority for MVP:** Fiona (volume, social virality) + Gary/Gloria (revenue). Others become content verticals.

## Jobs-to-be-done (feature map)

| Job | Feature |
|---|---|
| "Help me decide *where* I can actually go with my budget" | Budget-first destination finder; "first trip friendly" scores |
| "Tell me when a flight is cheap" | Fare tracker + deal alerts (Going-style) from home airport |
| "Tell me exactly what to do, in order" | Personalized trip-readiness checklist (passport, ETA/ETIAS, vaccines, insurance, phone/eSIM, money, adapters) |
| "Help me not panic at the airport" | Airport walkthroughs, "what happens at customs" explainers, layover guides |
| "Help me talk to people" | Survival-phrase language packs by destination; pronunciation practice; offline phrasebook |
| "Show me the famous stuff *and* the real stuff" | Touristy / Off-the-path dual lists per city, vetted |
| "What if something goes wrong?" | Emergency card: embassy, local 911 equivalent, insurance hotline, STEP enrollment |
| "I need a human" | Chat/phone with trained travel guides (premium tier) |

## Segmentation by trip type

- **First overseas trip** (core) — highest anxiety, highest guidance value.
- **Mexico/Canada/Caribbean "starter trips"** — easiest on-ramp; many first-timers start here.
- **VFR trips** — underserved by mainstream OTAs, high repeat rate.
- **Group/family trips** — one planner organizing for many; viral loop.
