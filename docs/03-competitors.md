# 03 — Competitors

> **Positioning gap:** Nobody owns *"the confident first trip abroad."* Booking giants own transactions, deal newsletters own fares, language apps own lessons, guidebooks own content — the first-timer has to stitch 8–12 tools together. Our product is the **guided, all-in-one on-ramp**.

## Landscape by category

### A. Online travel agencies (OTAs) — booking
| Player | Strength | Weakness vs. us |
|---|---|---|
| **Expedia Group** (Expedia, Hotels.com, Vrbo) | Scale, loyalty (One Key), B2B (30% of revenue in 2024 — powers Chase Travel, Walmart) | Transactional, no hand-holding; not built for nervous novices |
| **Booking Holdings** (Booking.com, Priceline, Kayak, Agoda, OpenTable) | Global lodging (8M+ alt-lodging listings), AI trip planner | Same — inventory, not guidance |
| **Hopper** | Mobile-first, price prediction, fintech add-ons (price freeze, cancel-for-any-reason), HTS B2B | Deal/fintech focused; customer-service complaints; little destination guidance |
| **Trip.com** | Asia strength, low prices | Low brand trust among U.S. novices |

**Implication:** Partner, don't compete — earn affiliate commission from them (see `04-startup-and-operating-costs.md`).

### B. Metasearch & fare tools
| Player | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Google Flights** | Free, excellent price tracking & calendar — our *biggest substitute* for fare alerts. We must add value on top (curation + "is this a good deal for a beginner?" + full trip plan). |
| **Kayak / Skyscanner / Momondo** | Price alerts, explore maps; ad/CPC-funded |

### C. Flight deal subscriptions
| Player | Pricing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Going** (formerly Scott's Cheap Flights) | Free tier; Premium ~$49/yr; Elite ~$199/yr *(verify)* | Closest model to our fare product; proves Americans pay for deal alerts. No trip guidance or language. |
| **Thrifty Traveler Premium**, **Dollar Flight Club** | ~$50–$100/yr | Similar; some points/miles focus |

### D. Trip planning / AI itinerary
| Player | Notes |
|---|---|
| **TripIt, Wanderlog, Roadtrippers** | Itinerary organization; assume user already knows what to do |
| **Layla, Mindtrip, ChatGPT / Gemini / Claude** | General AI planners — commoditizing itinerary generation. Our moat must be *trust, verified local content, and human backup*, not "AI itinerary." |
| **Airbnb Experiences, GetYourGuide, Viator (TripAdvisor)** | Tours/activities marketplaces — affiliate partners |

### E. Content / guidance
| Player | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Rick Steves** | Strong trust with 50+ Americans; tours + guidebooks + TV. The closest *spirit* to us for Persona 2. |
| **Lonely Planet, Fodor's, Atlas Obscura** | Guidebooks / hidden gems content |
| **Nomadic Matt, travel YouTubers/TikTokers** | Free, fragmented, entertainment-first |
| **Reddit r/travel, r/solotravel** | Free peer advice; high trust, low structure |
| **State Dept travel.state.gov, STEP** | Authoritative but dry; we should link/integrate, not replace |

### F. Language learning
| Player | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Duolingo** | 12.2M paid subscribers end of 2025; ~$1.04B revenue (+39%). Gamified, long-horizon — not trip-specific. |
| **Babbel, Pimsleur, Rosetta Stone, Promova** | Babbel/Promova actively publish "travel anxiety" research — they see the same opportunity. |
| **Google Translate / camera translate** | Free substitute for in-the-moment needs |

**Our angle:** "Survival language in 2 weeks for *your* trip" — destination-specific phrase packs, not a full course. Partner/affiliate with Babbel/Pimsleur for people who want more.

### G. Human travel advisors & host agencies
- ~Traditional & home-based advisors (often under host agencies like Fora, KHM, Avoya). Fora has modernized the model with tech + creator-advisors.
- Strength: personal trust. Weakness: usually serve higher-budget trips; first-timer on $2K budget is unprofitable for them.
- **Opportunity:** a premium "talk to a guide" tier, or partner with a host agency for complex bookings.

### H. Group / guided tour operators for young or first-time travelers
- **EF Ultimate Break, Contiki, Intrepid, G Adventures, Rick Steves Tours** — solve fear by bundling everything. Our alternative: *independent travel with a safety net*. Affiliate partners for those who prefer a tour.

## Threats from incumbents
- **Google** could add "first-trip" guidance to Flights/Maps cheaply.
- **Expedia/Booking AI assistants** improving quickly; OpenAI/Anthropic integrations (e.g., Amadeus building connections into Claude, Sept 2026) mean AI agents get booking access.
- **Duolingo** could launch travel mode / partner with an OTA.

## Defensibility levers
1. **Brand of reassurance** — tone, community, trusted creators.
2. **Proprietary verified content** — local contributors, "first-timer tested" itineraries, updated entry requirements.
3. **Community / buddy network** — first-timers matched with experienced "travel mentors."
4. **Lifecycle ownership** — from passport application to post-trip "plan the next one."
5. **Data** — what novices actually search, fear and book.
